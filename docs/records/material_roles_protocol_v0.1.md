# Material and environment role overlay v0.1

21 October 2026, ARIADNE stage24. Separate versioned, source-label role proposals
over the frozen stage23 audit; no new model calls or scientific admission.

## Boundary and inputs

Preserve the existing RecordCandidate schema, raw fields, candidate identities,
checks, review states, native responses and all prior execution code. Introduce
only a new standalone projection schema, records_material_roles_v0.1. It is not
a database migration, corrected candidate, replacement extraction or review action.
Use every candidate version and every source slot declared by the verified parent.

Verify parent input/code/PDF/output hashes, the parent report manifest, source
registry and exact source/candidate selections. Regenerate the parent's relation
map from its frozen page geometry before projection. Preserve the parent report
byte for byte as a local artifact. Failure or drift stops export with diagnostics.

## Role policy

Every projection contains exactly these five roles:

1. plasticiser_components: printed component code/order or complete group heading;
2. plasticiser_composition: a printed row ratio under the plasticiser header;
3. plasticiser_basis: the printed mass-fraction basis for that column;
4. membrane_composition: unknown in this policy, without asserted bindings;
5. measurement_environment: unknown in this policy, without asserted bindings.

Classify actual selected source occurrences from legacy medium,
medium_composition and composition_basis. The source field name and model note
do not establish a role. A field can map to another role when its exact source
coordinates support that role. Preserve every selection, its original field,
its literal text and the separately identified reference/context spans.

A unique literal plasticiser header is required. Component code/group proposals
use the parent's row/group geometry; unknown, tied or shared row scope stays
unsupported. A ratio proposal additionally requires a unique printed fraction
basis and recognized mixture group, exact row-cell coordinates and literal
decimal-slash syntax. Do not parse, normalize, sum or convert ratios. Do not
infer fraction=1 from SL/EC/G2-like pure-solvent codes, W mass percentages or a
model's claim of purity. Preserve printed component order without chemistry lookup.

The exact complete same-group heading may map as complete_group_heading while
retaining its selected prefix and a separate component reference. This explicitly
documented new role rule does not rewrite stage23's tighter-coordinate findings.
A basis selection may retain its printed parentheses as a boundary variant;
partial words, other columns and identical text elsewhere do not substitute.

Missing legacy fields remain missing and are not filled from available source
cells. Unassigned selections retain exact evidence and diagnostics. Multiple
legacy selections for one source role are retained, not silently merged or ranked.
Unclassified preparation/conditioning/method text is outside this role policy.
In particular, preparation atmosphere and general method text do not establish
measurement environment. Printed plasticiser composition cannot establish final
membrane composition or the experimental environment.

Every populated role is only source_label_proposal, never scientifically
confirmed. All scientific_support/scientific_admission states stay not_evaluated.
Unknown membrane/environment roles cannot carry bindings. Do not require manual
per-case resolutions or introduce writable decision forms.

## Validation and review

unassigned selections and per-candidate projections. Validate exact text, source
membership, article membership, field/status consistency and old candidate hash.
Reject schema extras, duplicate/missing roles and unsupported scientific states.

Test pure code versus ratio, repeated ratios at different coordinates, reordered
groups, complete-heading/basis boundaries, mass percent versus fraction basis,
missing or shared context, unrelated method/preparation text, misleading field
names/notes, missing selections, duplicate source-role bindings, source drift and
scientific-status contradictions. Run targeted and full regression checks.

alongside the old fields, previous audit, original PDF links, exact source text,

baseline. Independent scientific accuracy, specimen identity, method applicability,
whole-article completeness and environment extraction remain open.
