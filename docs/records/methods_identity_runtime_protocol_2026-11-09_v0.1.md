the read-only Methods identity audit. No real model/OAuth calls, slots, DB writes,

## Policy and scope

Add an opt-in `RAG_METHODS_IDENTITY_POLICY=ordered_methods_identity_v0.1` policy;
default `disabled` preserves current behavior. Keep old complete fact/quotation,
explicit-marker assembly, English and Extractive paths. Never alter registered
source types or fetch evidence outside original retrieval/filters/budgets.

The new finite path requires exactly three distinct raw parents, one resolved
author/year work and one explicitly recorded text layer: front-matter Abstract
and Introduction, bounded Material Preparation, bounded Conclusion. The Methods
parent may have its existing main_text, formula_block or figure_caption label;
the two result parents must be main_text. Verify bounded internal Methods text,
not the whole-parent classifier. Reject caption/table markers inside that span.

Recompute full witness graph from raw sources: explicit solvent/additive aliases,
three solvent/ratio/base-code entries with respectively; three additive doses and
product codes with separately/to obtain; literal Conclusion target; full Abstract
composition, capacity and CE and matching Conclusion CE. Compare salt, base dose,
all solvents, volume ratio, additive, selected dose and reported sum. Codes are
opaque; preserve structural hyphens with raw NFKC/whitespace-only comparison.
Ordered dose/product pairing is an explicitly selected policy assumption, not
general scientific ownership. Preserve unknown conditions/status and units.
No transfer of measurement conditions from preparation or between experiments.

Only after all checks may the existing F1..F3 contract be offered. Keep every
parent, including definition-only Methods, in the request-local graph. Serialize
original source_type/page/hash/layer bindings; rebuild the entire graph at every
selection validation. Reject partial/altered graph, raw source, facts, role,
policy or reference. Send exact definition quotes with addresses to the model.
Server-generated Russian rendering and final citations must expose definition
parents and explicitly state the ordered-list assumption. UI must show readable
definitions and original PDF links, including the definition-only parent.
Required-concept and required-relation vetoes remain effective.
