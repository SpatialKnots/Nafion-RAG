## Prospective scope and hypothesis

Stage26 exposed a limitation of the unchanged stage25 Russian literal policy.
Its two post-hoc English source examples are development inputs to this new
finite grammar, not independent test data. Hypothesis: additional explicit RU/EN
wording can produce bounded source-scope proposals while retaining abstentions.
Proposal counts do not measure scientific accuracy or condition recall.

Before running the new policy on real article blocks, freeze this protocol, new
code, constructed tests, all inherited input/code/PDF/output hashes and the exact
stage26 source packet and report. Preparation must not execute either policy.
Run only constructed source cases before preparation. No tuning after real output
inspection; a policy change requires a separate version, protocol and preparation.

Reuse **every** stage26 transfer page and proven registered block: two primary
task groups, three PDFs including the proposed supplement, 52 pages / 851 blocks.
Unproven mappings remain deferred with source text and diagnostics. The Kayumov
control is separate from transfer counts. Full registered blocks are not assumed
to be complete scientific paragraphs or independent experiments.

## Finite policy and source boundaries

The new module is additive; stage25/26 functions, candidates, fields, source text,
PDFs, database/review store and prior outputs are immutable. No model/API calls,
new measurement records, automatic admissions, field projection or unit parsing.

Match literal RU cues for conductivity, cell preparation/setup, before-measurement
holding and impedance. Match literal English passive conductivity measurements,
measurements performed/carried out/conducted, impedance spectra recorded/measured,
sample preparation/casting/drying/annealing/synthesis, equilibration wording and
explicit before-measurement holding. Whitespace and an explicitly listed
`equilibrat- ... ing` break may match across lines without altering any character.
Unsupported paraphrases and other languages remain unsupported.

Keep sentence-like units split at punctuation before uppercase letters, digits,
an opening parenthesis or source end. Suppress a finite list of abbreviation
boundaries; decimals are not boundaries. Preserve exact absolute Unicode offsets,
source region/page/layer/PDF identity, every cue and the complete unchanged text.
Never join blocks/pages, repair hyphenation, infer table/sample applicability or
infer environment/equilibrium completion from protocol wording.

A lowercase/non-uppercase initial block boundary or missing terminal sentence
punctuation is unproven and withholds a proposal. This is a syntactic heuristic,
not proof of paragraph completeness. Earlier layout/graphics diagnostics remain.

Literal negation, reported-work/citation and modal/future guards are deliberately
**block-wide**. A guard anywhere in a block withholds all otherwise eligible
proposals in that block, preserving its exact span even outside the current
clause. This knowingly over-abstains; it does not resolve semantic negation scope
or establish own-work origin when no guard is found. Finite guards include English
not/never/without/neither/no/cannot/contractions, Russian не/без/ни, reported/
previous/literature/according to/et al., Russian ранее/согласно/по данным, numeric
bracket citations and modal/future words. Other attribution forms are unsupported.

Preserve all matched kinds. Distinct measurement/preparation/conditioning kinds
in a clause yield ambiguous_scope, with no selected role. Multiple cues of the
same kind remain visible. State precedence: incomplete_source, guarded_source,
ambiguous_scope, scope_proposal, unsupported_clause. All guards/cues remain even
when an earlier state takes precedence. A proposal needs one distinct kind,
proven syntactic boundaries and zero block guards. Scientific support and
per-result applicability remain not_evaluated/not_established.
