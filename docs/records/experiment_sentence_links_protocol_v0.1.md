# Offline adjacent statement dependencies v0.1

Scope: frozen eight-case requested_context_links_20261029/review_v04 and the
separately versioned constructed fixture set. No API/OAuth, DB, embeddings,
retrieval or production change. Preserve prior code/contracts/manifests/slots.

Propose only explicit adjacent dependencies within one supplied evidence chunk:
an anaphoric measurement reference (this/same measurement; этим измерением) to a
single preceding measurement operation, or an impedance instrument statement
followed by recorded spectra. Sentence and semicolon-clause links must be
reported separately. Adjacency alone is insufficient; do not skip intervening
statements or carry durations, temperatures or sample identities across units.

Block proposal on paragraph boundaries, preparation/drying/soaking cues, competing
sample/property/method cues, mixed sample/method contexts, negation/hypothesis,
other-work references and nonunique exact source anchors. An accepted template
is still a dependency proposal, not scientific entailment or answer approval.
Unknown sample identity stays explicit. No generic coreference is claimed.

Each parent/child and bridge cue retains raw offsets and unique original-page
anchors with article/chunk/citation, selected layer/layer ID and original PDF
path/hash. No dehyphenation or whitespace repair in saved text. Attach the graph
as a sidecar to previous quantity/context diagnostics; never remove their
unresolved statuses or transform an equilibrium criterion into an exact time.

Measure proposal errors and unresolved/blocked cases on the constructed fixtures
with their declared assistant-authored expectations. These are exposed software
regressions, not held-out accuracy or independent annotation. Keep full frozen
cases, searchable HTML/TAC for all source documents and all intermediate results.
Preserve original HTTP/format/provider-filter results. General semantic coverage,
independent evaluation, partial-answer policy and runtime admission remain open.
