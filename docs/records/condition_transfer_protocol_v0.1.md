inventory and source metadata. A proven registered PDF block is one probe unit.

and finite positive boxes. An unproven mapping remains a deferred page with full
source and explicit diagnostics, not a silently omitted page or guessed unit.

local raw rule probe on a registered block is not activating an old deferred page

stage25 source_events. Preserve every returned unsupported or incomplete event,

absence of experimental conditions. Event scientific support remains not_evaluated;
