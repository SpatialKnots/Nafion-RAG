# Context completeness audit v0.1 — stage39, 24 October 2026

Written before new audit outcomes. Frozen scope: all four stage38 packets, all
their exact selected blocks/windows/dependencies and all ten TAC documents.
The stage38 compact wire and its complete diagnostic authority remain immutable.
No retrieval, database, inference, PDF alteration or external lookup is allowed.

The additive local audit explains each full-page occurrence using independently
recomputed registered intervals: core-block union, original-window union, current
selected-node union, single-window coverage and dependency-only additions. It
accounts for every registered page and each retained node. Full-page guards and
all inherited refusals remain unchanged. Removing duplicate window references
cannot remove text required by the union of all original windows. No automatic
selection/removal or smaller replacement payload is constructed in this stage.

Every original source event receives an explicit record with its complete exact
context, block/source identity, unchanged cue/guard/boundary information, and
finite literal inventories for property, sample mention, method and conditions.
Property families reuse the stage28 PROPERTY_CUES unchanged. The new local finite
inventories recognize explicit membrane/sample/electrode mentions, impedance/DSC
method phrases, numeric temperature/time/frequency mentions and explicit argon/
nitrogen/air/vacuum mentions. Matches retain exact original text and absolute
Unicode offsets. No unit normalization, numeric interpretation, sentence repair,
anaphora resolution, inferred sample identity or own-work origin is introduced.

A source-cooccurrence proposal requires one literal property family, a sample
mention, a method mention and a condition mention in the SAME complete event,
an unchanged measurement_protocol/measurement_setup scope_proposal, no original
guards or boundary diagnostics, no conflicting preparation/conditioning cues and
no explicit preparation indicator in that event. This is a traceable proposal,
not evidence of scientific entailment, sample identity or per-result applicability.
DSC method mentions are incompatible with a conductivity-only property family;
impedance method mentions are incompatible with a thermal-only family. Mixed
families remain unresolved. Preparation and conditioning events receive their
own literal inventories and are never copied to measurement conditions. All
unsupported, guarded, clipped and missing-field cases remain explicit and unlinked.

Unit inventories expose co-located event IDs and distinct property families but
do not join sentences, assign missing fields across events, propagate conditions
across blocks/pages/articles/layers or promote structural connectivity to science.
Every dependency and every open page boundary receives an explicit disposition.
Unresolved source-order/cross-page edges stay unresolved; protected groups keep
their original membership and do not establish table semantics. No absent neighbor
is fetched. Exact source text and all preexisting events/guards stay available.

Before run, freeze code/tests/protocol, complete stage38 bindings and source-bound
assistant development checks. These checks are exposed development examples, not
independent scientific gold. They include direct conductivity cooccurrence,
separate cell preparation in argon, incomplete impedance and equilibrium fragments,
protected table retention and the three preexisting no-refusal-upgrade cases.
Constructed tests additionally challenge negation, citation, modality, mixed
properties, same-block condition borrowing and foreign source/reference drift.

Prepare/run/export use exclusive output directories, source/hash verification,
current PDF registration and deterministic replay. Results include per-question
coverage, event dispositions, every boundary, checks and all old blockers. Report
counts as occurrences within the four questions; repeated evidence is not new
independent support. No scientific accuracy or improved recall/answer quality is
claimed. network_ready, generation_performed and external_request_constructed
remain false even for a complete literal-cooccurrence proposal.

HTML provides search, full expandable source contexts, source PDF links, field
inventories, reasons for abstention, all coverage/boundary details and separate
eight-column TAC review for all ten documents. Historical TAC, proposed metadata,
unknowns and unimplemented Terms remain explicitly labeled. No mandatory human
per-case resolution or writable Terms workflow is added.
