# Offline answer scope audit v0.1

30 October 2026. Development protocol for step 2 of the answer-quality plan.
This protocol is frozen before the first run of the new audit.

Inputs: the unchanged saved swelling question, structurally valid saved claims,
their five supplied evidence chunks, eight registered pages, four separately
archived post-hoc pages, and the three original article metadata records. Reuse
the twelve saved event-ownership cases and their three saved model proposals as
a separate operation-ownership replay, without manufacturing events from answer
prose. Original PDFs, raw provider/API output and all previous manifests remain
immutable. New network/API/OAuth/DB operations and slot reservations: zero.

The new audit recognises a finite RU/EN grammar: maximum/property/form mentions,
explicit Celsius points/ranges, room-temperature wording, medium/scope cues,
alpha assignments and number-for-Li/H-form relations. Every detected source
fragment retains exact raw page offsets, original PDF identity and text layer.
Mapped dehyphenation may aid recognition but must never rewrite source text.
Unsupported wording and table columns remain unparsed/unresolved.

Requirements:

- Report the comparison universe as unspecified or lexically restricted; never
  infer corpus completeness from selected evidence or a maximum word.
- Do not equate room temperature with 25°C, a range with a point, or percent
  with dimensionless alpha. Do not convert units automatically.
- Associate a numeric value with a form only through the supported explicit
  syntax. H-form values must not become Li-form values. A form label alone does
  not establish the material or swelling-property identity.
- Keep statement/operation regions and medium/temperature/basis cues as
  diagnostic locality only. Same-statement/region co-occurrence is unverified;
  do not transfer conditions from another sentence, caption, table, work or
  operation. Mixed forms, media, basis cues, references and negation are unresolved.
- Separate nearest preceding regression/experimental cues from a proven value
  basis. They are proposals with exact anchors, not scientific classifications.
- Report larger same-form numeric candidates as review candidates only, scoped
  separately to the original supplied evidence and post-hoc pages. They do not
  automatically prove a counterexample, comparable conditions or a new maximum.
- Audit numeric claim surfaces using only their actual citation IDs. Unknown IDs
  or malformed source identity fail explicitly; absence of detected patterns is
  not proof of source absence. No repair of old claim/status/citations.
- No pass/admission/answerable=true output: scientific coverage remains
  unresolved or outside finite scope. No runtime integration in this iteration.

Software controls cover wrong form/property/temperature/unit, negation, ranges,
mixed/adjacent operations, multiple conditions, caption-only temperature,
regression versus observation, source identity/offset integrity, unrelated
citations, duplicate IDs and deterministic replay. These constructed controls
and the familiar real question are exposed development evidence, not independent
accuracy. Preserve false flags and unresolved cases; do not tune to case IDs.

Artifacts: versioned JSON, code/input hashes and snapshots; local HTML with all
five works in the union of swelling and event scopes, full expandable available
TAC, search, original PDF/page links and explicit unknown/uncollected fields.
Operation replays retain original formal verdicts. Completion of this bounded
offline diagnostic is not completion of semantic ownership validation or
independent scientific acceptance.
