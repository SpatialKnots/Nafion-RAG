The existing explicit claim checker validates caller-supplied tuples, not free
prose. This module separately implements four bounded raw-source contracts:

1. Negative identification status versus affirmative identification of the same
   locally bound chemical/product subject; finite bilingual identity vocabulary,
   negation and hedged assertions stay separate. A possible candidate is not an
   established identification. Unbound pronouns/other subjects stay uninspected.
2. CEI/SEI composition ownership: exact chemical-token fingerprint linked by
   explicit composition syntax or a bounded XPS/these-species witness graph.
   Role mismatch requires one unambiguous cited-source role for that fingerprint.
   Merely seeing SEI/CEI in a mixed chunk is insufficient. Multiple-role/comparative
   claims or conflicting source owners remain unresolved, not accepted/block-listed.
3. Mass denominator for explicitly source-bound Wh/kg energy reported on combined
   cathode/anode masses. Preserve quantities/units, no unit conversion or gold values.
4. Co-solvent qualifier for explicitly source-bound cycling-efficiency data of
   PREFIX–COMPONENT mixtures when question/answer names the component and repeats
   the percentage. No transferring mixture performance to a pure solvent silently.

No authors/years/article IDs/pages/reference values/case IDs in scientific rules.
All findings carry citation/article/chunk/source-type/raw SHA/offsets and exact
text. Compare only actually cited source IDs, preserve ambiguities and unknowns.
Require raw evidence, not derivative fact records. Cross-source conflicting status/
roles or unsupported grammar must never become scientific confirmation.

wrapper returns an abstention on a detected bounded conflict/gap, preserves provider

does not make another provider call. Existing generation validation stays.
