Detailed assistant source review, not independent scientific acceptance:

- Dispersion: duration is exactly `2 часа`, with the correct working-temperature
  and equilibrium fragments. The `storage` kind does not establish the intended
  measurement-wait role. `sample = ячейку` identifies the cell rather than a resolved
  material identity. Only one event is returned; the spectra-registration event
  and the explicit `После выдержки` relation are absent.
- Criterion: duration is `not_found`; `по неиз-\nменности спектров` is selected as
  the completion criterion. The operation spans the long spectra/wait/criterion
  clause instead of a short atomic operation. Conditions include interleaved
  formula/layout text from the unchanged source layer. It remains visible, not
  repaired or interpreted as a clean condition.
- Protonation: the target operation is exactly `Про-\nтонирование`, but its
  duration selects `2 ч` from the earlier H2O2-cleaning sentence. The HCl operation
  has no reported duration in this excerpt. The separate drying event selects
  the same `2 ч` and `при 60–80°C`, both from cleaning. The source instead gives
  drying `1 ч` at `60°C`, then separate storage for `2 недели`. Both selected
  durations are labeled `at_least`, without a lower-bound cue in their selected
  text. Only protonation and drying are returned; cleaning/rinsing/storage are
  not separately represented.
- Diagnostics are `Ф`, `Ф`, `Д`. They satisfy the nonblank structural definition
  but provide no requested explanation. The schema/parser are not loosened or
  edited after observing them.
- All event-relation lists are empty. Scientific target identity, completeness,
  relations and ownership remain unverified by the local parser. The original
  v0.1 raw protonation target left duration missing; the new raw target borrows
  cleaning time. These exposed observations do not establish a causal ranking
  of prompts/models or an independent accuracy difference.

Limitations:

Three exposed development examples with assistant-authored preliminary labels;
no independent annotation, reviewer, held-out evaluation or accuracy estimate.
Source integrity and JSON schema can both pass a scientifically wrong assignment.
Sparse exact-anchor comparison leaves alternative/ambiguous operations unscored
and does not test all values, target semantics or relations. No semantic runtime
gate or independent scientific acceptance is implemented. Earlier provider-filter
trigger and ORM/cache limitations remain open; their historical failures are
preserved. None of this pilot's formal-validity results authorizes deployment.

Verdict:

The limited live stage is complete: three formally/source-exact valid replies,
but critical cross-operation borrowing remains. Scientific extraction readiness
is not established. Next work is offline design of operation/value ownership
validation and separate independent evaluation. `/answer` remains unchanged;
all three new live slots and every older budget remain closed.
